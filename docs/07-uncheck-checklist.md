# Chapter 7 · The Uncheck Checklist

*Time: 10 minutes with this page open and Windows Settings in the other window*

This is the whole guide, compressed into one list of boxes. Run it top to bottom —
it's ordered by Settings page so you're not bouncing around. Each line names the
box/toggle, where it lives, and why you'd uncheck it. If a row doesn't exist on
your machine: congratulations, there's nothing to do for it. Move on.

## A. While you're at it: settings-sync & passwords

| ✅ | Where | What to toggle | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ☐ | Settings > Accounts > Windows backup | **"Remember my preferences"** — turn **off** (and the **Passwords** sub-row in particular) | Stops settings (and saved passwords) syncing to your Microsoft account between PCs |
| ☐ | Settings > Accounts > Windows backup | **"Remember my apps"** — off if you want a clean slate on new machines | Cosmetic (app-list restoration), zero other effect |

## B. OneDrive folder auto-send

| ✅ | Where | What to toggle | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ☐ | OneDrive icon (taskbar) > gear > **Settings** > **Sync and backup** > **Manage backup** | **Desktop / Documents / Pictures** tiles that you want **local-only** | Signed-in PCs often back these folders up automatically. Choose **"Keep files on this device"** when you stop |
| ☐ | Same OneDrive Settings window, **Account** tab (optional) | **Unlink this PC** | Total stop for OneDrive sync on this machine. Reversible |
| ☐ | Photos app > gear | **"Show cloud gallery from OneDrive"** (wording varies) — **off** | So Photos doesn't blend in your cloud photo library without your say-so |
| ☐ | Word/Excel/PowerPoint > **File** > **Options** > **Save** | **"Save to Computer by default"** — checked | New Office documents stop defaulting straight to OneDrive |

## C. Copilot itself

| ✅ | Where | What to toggle | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ☐ | Copilot > profile icon > **Settings** | Rows you don't use: **voice/mic**, plugins you can't explain | Nothing breaks; fewer features, fewer permissions |
| ☐ | Copilot > profile icon > **Settings** (or history list) | **Delete all activity** once a month | Resets your conversation history in the cloud |
| ☐ | Copilot > profile icon > **Settings** > **Send on Enter / Auto-send** | **Off** if half-finished messages keep flying | You press an explicit **Send** button instead |
| ☐ | Settings > Personalization > **Copilot key** | Point the keyboard's Copilot key at **Windows Search** if you prefer | Reversible, per-preference |

## D. Windows-level privacy (quick tour)

| ✅ | Where | What to toggle | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ☐ | Settings > Privacy & security > **General** | **"Show suggested content in settings app"** — **off** | Less in-app advertising posing as advice |
| ☐ | Settings > Privacy & security > General | **"Let websites provide locally relevant content…"** — off if you're minimizing | Trims a little cloud chatter |
| ☐ | Settings > Privacy & security > General | **Tailored experiences** — **off** | Stops Microsoft using account data to target you with tips/offers |
| ☐ | Settings > Privacy & security > **Search permissions** | **Cloud content search** — **off** | Start search no longer looks inside your cloud account. Local search unaffected |
| ☐ | Settings > Privacy & security > **Speech** | Off, unless you use voice typing daily | Voice typing still works offline, just slightly less polished |
| ☐ | Settings > Privacy & security > **Microphone** | If you never use voice features, deny per-app | The word *microphone* is load-bearing; keep it close |

## E. Recall (Copilot+ PCs only — skip entirely if Section 5.1 found no Recall)

| ✅ | Where | What to toggle | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ☐ | Settings > Privacy & security > **Recall & snapshots** > **Save snapshots** | **Off** if you don't want a screen timeline stored at all | Then also click **Delete all snapshots** on the same page — unchecking is only half the job |
| ☐ | Same page (if snapshot is staying on) | **Filter apps** — add your banking, health, and password apps; **Filter websites** (if offered) | Guardrails so the real sensitive windows aren't in the timeline |
| ☐ | Same page (if snapshot is staying on) | Lower the **retention days** to the smallest number you're comfortable with | Fewer days on disk, smaller blast radius |
| ☐ | Recall app itself | Remember **Pause** for sensitive moments and **Delete all snapshots** anytime | The two safety valves |

## F. Apps that think they're the main character

| ✅ | Where | What to toggle | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ☐ | **Settings > Apps** > **Installed apps** > per-app (**⋮** menu) > **App permissions** | Deny Location, Mic, Camera for apps that don't need them | Windows 11 has a per-app permission page your phone has been training you for, since forever |
| ☐ | Microsoft Store > your profile > **Settings** | **"Video autoplay"** — off | The Store shouldn't make noise when you're just looking for an update |

## How to use this list for the long run

1. **Do it now**, all at once (20 minutes with coffee), and tick the checkboxes above
   as a personal record.
2. **Re-run it** after every Windows *feature update* (Settings > Windows Update >
   "Update history" is where you'd learn you got one), because updates have been
   known to re-enable a switch or two with a firm but anonymous apology.
3. **Skip rows that don't exist** on your PC — the list is intentionally
   comprehensive on purpose so this page works on as many Windows 11 editions
   (Home, Pro, Copilot+, and on) as possible.

That's it. Take a breath: the computer now shows *you* what you chose.
