Chapter 7 · The Uncheck Checklist

Time: 10 minutes with this page open and Windows Settings in the other window

This is the whole guide, compressed into one list of boxes. Run it top to bottom — it's ordered by Settings page so you're not bouncing around. Each line names the box/toggle, where it lives, and why you'd uncheck it. If a row doesn't exist on your machine: congratulations, there's nothing to do for it. Move on.

A. While you're at it: settings-sync & passwords

✅WhereWhat to toggleWhy
☐Settings > Accounts > Windows backup"Remember my preferences" — turn off (and the Passwords sub-row in particular)Stops settings (and saved passwords) syncing to your Microsoft account between PCs
☐Settings > Accounts > Windows backup"Remember my apps" — off if you want a clean slate on new machinesCosmetic (app-list restoration), zero other effect

B. OneDrive folder auto-send

✅WhereWhat to toggleWhy
☐OneDrive icon (taskbar) > gear > Settings > Sync and backup > Manage backupDesktop / Documents / Pictures tiles that you want local-onlySigned-in PCs often back these folders up automatically. Choose "Keep files on this device" when you stop
☐Same OneDrive Settings window, Account tab (optional)Unlink this PCTotal stop for OneDrive sync on this machine. Reversible
☐Photos app > gear"Show cloud gallery from OneDrive" (wording varies) — offSo Photos doesn't blend in your cloud photo library without your say-so
☐Word/Excel/PowerPoint > File > Options > Save"Save to Computer by default" — checkedNew Office documents stop defaulting straight to OneDrive

C. Copilot itself

✅WhereWhat to toggleWhy
☐Copilot > profile icon > SettingsRows you don't use: voice/mic, plugins you can't explainNothing breaks; fewer features, fewer permissions
☐Copilot > profile icon > Settings (or history list)Delete all activity once a monthResets your conversation history in the cloud
☐Copilot > profile icon > Settings > Send on Enter / Auto-sendOff if half-finished messages keep flyingYou press an explicit Send button instead
☐Settings > Personalization > Copilot keyPoint the keyboard's Copilot key at Windows Search if you preferReversible, per-preference

D. Windows-level privacy (quick tour)

✅WhereWhat to toggleWhy
☐Settings > Privacy & security > General"Show suggested content in settings app" — offLess in-app advertising posing as advice
☐Settings > Privacy & security > General"Let websites provide locally relevant content…" — off if you're minimizingTrims a little cloud chatter
☐Settings > Privacy & security > GeneralTailored experiences — offStops Microsoft using account data to target you with tips/offers
☐Settings > Privacy & security > Search permissionsCloud content search — offStart search no longer looks inside your cloud account. Local search unaffected
☐Settings > Privacy & security > SpeechOff, unless you use voice typing dailyVoice typing still works offline, just slightly less polished
☐Settings > Privacy & security > MicrophoneIf you never use voice features, deny per-appThe word microphone is load-bearing; keep it close

E. Recall (Copilot+ PCs only — skip entirely if Section 5.1 found no Recall)

✅WhereWhat to toggleWhy
☐Settings > Privacy & security > Recall & snapshots > Save snapshotsOff if you don't want a screen timeline stored at allThen also click Delete all snapshots on the same page — unchecking is only half the job
☐Same page (if snapshot is staying on)Filter apps — add your banking, health, and password apps; Filter websites (if offered)Guardrails so the real sensitive windows aren't in the timeline
☐Same page (if snapshot is staying on)Lower the retention days to the smallest number you're comfortable withFewer days on disk, smaller blast radius
☐Recall app itselfRemember Pause for sensitive moments and Delete all snapshots anytimeThe two safety valves

F. Apps that think they're the main character

✅WhereWhat to toggleWhy
☐Settings > Apps > Installed apps > per-app (⋮ menu) > App permissionsDeny Location, Mic, Camera for apps that don't need themWindows 11 has a per-app permission page your phone has been training you for, since forever
☐Microsoft Store > your profile > Settings"Video autoplay" — offThe Store shouldn't make noise when you're just looking for an update

How to use this list for the long run

  1. Do it now, all at once (20 minutes with coffee), and tick the checkboxes above as a personal record.
  2. Re-run it after every Windows feature update (Settings > Windows Update > "Update history" is where you'd learn you got one), because updates have been known to re-enable a switch or two with a firm but anonymous apology.
  3. Skip rows that don't exist on your PC — the list is intentionally comprehensive on purpose so this page works on as many Windows 11 editions (Home, Pro, Copilot+, and on) as possible.

That's it. Take a breath: the computer now shows you what you chose.
