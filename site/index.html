Windows 11's Built-In AI: A Plain-English Walking Guide

By Asdale Dynamics, LLC · "Explained like a friend would."

A substantive, step-by-step manual for regular Windows 11 users who want to actually use the AI that ships with Windows — Copilot chat, the AI features in the Photos and Paint apps, and the newer, bigger privacy decisions (Recall, and the settings that quietly send things to the cloud).

Everything here is:

  • Plain English. No jargon left unexplained, no assumptions that you already know.
  • Step by step with words instead of screenshots. Every step includes a short "What you'll see on your screen" description, so you can follow along even if your screen looks a little different than described.
  • Privacy-first. This guide tells you honestly which features phone home, and gives you a plain checklist of exactly which boxes to uncheck (or turn off) if you'd rather keep things local.
  • No registry editing. Ever. Nothing in this guide asks you to open the Registry Editor or run any command-line voodoo. Everything is buttons, toggles, and menus.

How this is different from our other guides

  • New Windows computer, day one? Use our New Computer Setup Day Guide first — that one walks you through unboxing and first-time setup.
  • Copilot on your phone? That's our AI on Your Phone Guide.
  • This guide covers the AI that is already inside your Windows 11 PC — the one on your laptop or desktop keyboard, in your Photos app, in your Paint app, and (if your PC is newer) in Recall.

If you've already set the computer up and you're just discovering "wait, there's AI in here?" — you're in exactly the right place.

How to run this guide as a website

The manual lives as plain Markdown in docs/. A small, dependency-free Python script turns it into a static HTML site in site/.

python3 build.py        # renders docs/*.md into site/*.html
# then open site/index.html in any browser, or:
python3 -m http.server --directory site 8000   # preview at http://localhost:8000

No Node, no npm install, no internet connection needed. The generated site/ folder is fully self-contained (one CSS file, plain URLs) and can be copied anywhere.

Verification

Run the included check script — it rebuilds the site and verifies internal links, required pages, and footer credits:

python3 build.py --check
